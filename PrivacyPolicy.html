Privacy Policy for Alefin-Plus

Effective date: August 6, 2026 · App: Alefin-Plus (iOS)

Alefin-Plus is a personal planting notebook and field calculator. It helps you keep plot records, activity notes, season lanes, crop reference cards, and yield/seed/spacing math on the iPhone or iPad you already own. This policy explains what the app touches on your device and what it does not do.

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to the Alefin-Plus iOS application distributed on the App Store. The app runs on your device; planting records stay local unless you choose to export or share them yourself.

2. Data the app stores — on your device only

Content you enter stays in the app’s sandbox on that device (Documents / UserDefaults-style prefs). Typical items include:

  • Plot names, crop kinds, stages, soil notes, dimensions, cycle labels, and optional field photos
  • Activity log rows (sow / irrigate / fertilize / harvest and similar), quantities, costs, notes, season tags
  • Season-plan grouping derived from your plots
  • Calculator inputs and a short local history of saved results
  • Crop catalog entries you keep or edit (days to maturity, seed rate, yield references)
  • Settings such as Metric/Imperial, default crop, and default season

Alefin-Plus does not upload this material to our servers. We do not operate a backend that receives your plots or photos.

3. Camera and Photos

Camera and photo library access are optional and only used when you attach an image to a plot or an activity entry.

  • Camera — to capture a picture of a plot or field task for your own record
  • Photo Library — to choose an existing image to attach

Attached images are saved as files inside the app’s local photo folder on the device. Declining permission simply means you cannot attach photos; the rest of the notebook still works.

4. Sharing that you start yourself

From Settings you can export a plain-text snapshot of plots and activity logs. From the Activity Log you can share a filtered summary. Those flows use the system share sheet. Whatever leaves the device goes only to the destination you pick (Messages, Mail, Files, another app). Alefin-Plus does not silently forward that text.

5. What we do not collect

  • We do not ask for your name, phone number, or other identity details to use the app
  • No advertising SDK, attribution SDK, or in-app analytics pipeline in the current build
  • No location tracking for maps or weather
  • No continuous background upload of planting data
  • No sale of personal information

Apple may collect standard App Store / device diagnostics under Apple’s own terms when you install or use any App Store app. That is outside Alefin-Plus’s control.

6. Deleting your data

Inside Settings, Clear all data removes plots, logs, calculator history, catalog customizations, attached photos, and resets preferences to defaults on that device. You can also delete the app; iOS will remove the app sandbox with it.

7. Children

Alefin-Plus is a utility for gardening and field record-keeping. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly solicit personal information from children.

8. Changes

If a future release changes how Alefin-Plus handles data on or off the device, this page will be updated before that release ships, and the App Store listing will reflect the new data practices.
