Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026
App: AlefinBest (iOS)

Welcome to AlefinBest. We respect your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles privacy-related information. AlefinBest is a local casual game hub designed for offline play and on-device progress.

1. Information We Process

In ordinary use, AlefinBest does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, phone number, email address, precise location, contacts, or photo library.

The app may store the following gameplay-related data locally on your device:

  • Scores and progress (for example Best, Stars, wins, and recent summary)
  • Preference settings (for example sound, haptics, and tutorial flags)
  • Minimal local state needed for the app to function correctly

This data is kept on your device by default (for example via system local storage) to restore progress and preferences. It is not used to build advertising profiles.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve gameplay (scoring, retry, hub progress)
  • To remember your sound and haptics preferences
  • To keep the app stable and usable

We do not sell local gameplay data and do not use it for unrelated marketing profiling.

3. Third-Party Services and Tracking

The current version of AlefinBest focuses on on-device play. If a future version integrates analytics, advertising, or cloud sync, we will update this policy and provide choices or consent where required by applicable law.

4. Storage and Security

Local data remains on your device. Please protect your device and system account. Uninstalling the app or clearing app data may erase local progress and settings.

5. Children’s Privacy

AlefinBest is intended for general casual entertainment. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information, contact us using the support channel below and we will help address it.

6. Your Choices

  • Disable sound or haptics in the in-app Settings screen
  • Use system settings to manage permissions (the app will not use a permission it has not requested)
  • Uninstall the app to remove locally stored gameplay data

7. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Please review it periodically.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at AlefinBest@icloud.com, or through the support information on the App Store product page.
